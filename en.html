Frequently Asked Questions

What customers ask most about pricing, work and process

Do the package prices include labour and materials?

Yes. The listed packages include both labour and materials. Custom furniture is quoted separately after measuring because size, material and design vary.

How is the 400 sq ft reference price calculated?

The packages use a 400 sq ft two-bedroom flat as a reference. The final price depends on flat size, wall and floor condition and the scope of work, and is confirmed after photos or a site visit.

How do you repair concrete spalling?

We remove all loose concrete, apply a rust-proof coating, repair with professional repair mortar, then skim and repaint. Small areas usually take one to two days; larger areas need an on-site inspection.

Can the price change after work starts?

No. Once confirmed, the price does not change. The scope is stated in the quotation and any additional item is agreed with you first.

Do you work on public housing, HOS flats and private flats?

Yes, we work on all three and are familiar with estate management applications and handover requirements.

How long does a quotation take?

Send photos and the flat size on WhatsApp and we usually reply with an estimate the same day, without waiting for a site visit.
