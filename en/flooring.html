Frequently asked questions

What customers ask most about this work

Is SPC flooring waterproof?

The SPC core is waterproof, so mopping and splashes are fine. Standing water is not recommended, and it is not suitable for bathrooms.

Do I need to move furniture?

Yes. The work area needs to be clear, so large items should be moved or gathered into one room.

How long does it take?

A 400 sq ft flat usually takes 1 to 2 working days, depending on how much removal is needed.
