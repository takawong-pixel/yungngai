Flat Painting and Repainting in Hong Kong

Bubbling, peeling, mould or an outdated colour? Repainting is the fastest way to change how a flat feels. We have 10 years of residential painting experience across Hong Kong, and our own team does the work — no subcontracting.

Get a free WhatsApp quote

Two approaches, very different prices

Touch-up and repaint (from HK$9,800)

We do not strip the whole wall. We repair the problem areas such as cracks and mould, then skim and apply three coats throughout. Suitable when the existing substrate is still sound, or for a rental refresh. The old substrate is not stripped, so bubbling or flaking may return later.

Full skim coat and repaint (from HK$22,800)

We strip the old finish from walls and ceilings, apply a new skim coat, then repaint. More work and more time, but a far more durable result — suitable for a new home or long-term living.

Frequently asked questions

What customers ask most about this work

How long does painting take?

For a 400 sq ft flat, a touch-up and repaint usually takes 4 to 6 working days; a full skim coat takes about 7 to 10 working days because the skim needs to dry. Weather and flat condition affect this.

Do I need to move my furniture?

Move large items to the centre of a room or into one room. We cover floors and remaining furniture with protective sheets and remove them at the end.

Which paint do you use?

Standard interior emulsion, in the colour you choose. If you want a specific brand or low-VOC paint, tell us when requesting the quote.
