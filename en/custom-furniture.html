Custom Furniture: Wardrobes, Shoe Cabinets, TV Units

Small flats and awkward corners rarely suit off-the-shelf furniture. Custom-built pieces are measured to your flat, can run to the ceiling or into a recess, and can work around beams and sockets.

Why go custom

Done with the renovation

Frequently asked questions

What customers ask most about this work

How long does custom furniture take?

It depends on the number of pieces, materials and workshop schedule — usually several weeks from confirmed drawings, with timing explained at the measuring visit.

Can it match my renovation?

Yes. Finishes are matched to the walls and floor, and units are installed after painting to avoid damage.

What materials are available?

A range of boards, finishes and hardware; we recommend based on budget and how the piece will be used.
