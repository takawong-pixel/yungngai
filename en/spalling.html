Concrete Spalling Repair

Crumbling concrete, exposed rebar or falling fragments on a ceiling or wall should be dealt with quickly. Painting over it does not help: rusting rebar keeps expanding and the concrete will spall again.

What we do (from HK$5,000)

Removal and rust protection

We remove all loose concrete back to sound material, then apply a rust-proof coating to the exposed rebar to stop further corrosion.

Repair and reinstatement

The area is filled with professional repair mortar, then skimmed, sanded and repainted to match the surroundings.

Duration

Small repairs usually take 1 to 2 days. Larger areas need an on-site inspection to assess the extent of corrosion before quoting.

When to call someone

Rust stains or loose concrete

If you can see rebar or fragments are falling, treat it as urgent.

Bulging walls or widening cracks

These can mean the substrate has already failed; check before deciding the scope of work.

Frequently asked questions

What customers ask most about this work

Why does concrete spall?

Usually because rebar is rusting and expanding, or because the concrete cover is too thin and moisture reaches the steel. It gets worse if untreated.

Can I just paint over it?

Not advisable. Paint hides the surface while the underlying concrete stays loose and the steel keeps rusting, so it will fail again.

How long for large areas?

It depends on the extent and the condition of the rebar and may need staged work; the sequence is explained after inspection.
